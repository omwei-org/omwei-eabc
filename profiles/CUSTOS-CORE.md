## Why this profile is listed

This profile makes the exit-class register available for comparison. A second independently produced profile is what would test whether the vocabulary holds without either author having to explain their terms.

The register records four exit classes. In the referenced version, two are declared open and two are described as closed only within their stated boundaries. The register does not aggregate these statuses into a conformance level.

## Evidence boundary

The repository records that specific, pre-registered weakenings are detected under the stated test design. Those results do **not** establish execution-boundary non-bypassability. The linked Conformance Statement describes the implementation's findings and limitations; they remain attributable to that implementation and its author.

This directory entry is an index reference only. It does not independently assess the implementation, endorse its claims, or declare EABC conformance.
