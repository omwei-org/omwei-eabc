# Decisions

## Purpose

This directory contains decision records documenting decisions made by the EABC Specification Authority.

Decision records are **non-normative**. They document the authority's decisions and their rationale; they do not themselves constitute normative EABC specification text, redefine requirements, or replace the specification documents.

Normative changes resulting from an adopted decision are made separately in `docs/` through dedicated pull requests. This separation keeps authority decisions traceable without mixing decision records with the normative specification.

## Decision Record Requirements

A decision record SHOULD identify:

- decision ID and title;
- decision authority and authority holder;
- decision status and outcome;
- decision date;
- scope and adopted decision;
- rationale and consequences;
- amendments relative to the proposal, where applicable.
